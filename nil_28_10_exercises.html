2. Do you think prejudice against people from other nationalities is still a big problem today? Why or why not?
Yes, I think prejudice is still a problem in many neighborhoods. People sometimes treat others rudely just because they are from a different country. It is important to learn about other cultures directly.
3. Is it worth it to learn to play an instrument in an orchestra? What are the benefits?
I think it is worth it to play in an orchestra. The music is impressive and you meet many other musicians. You also learn to take care of your instrument and work well with others.

A Neighborhood Worth Caring For

Maria is the owner of a small business in her neighborhood. She sells food directly to her customers and always takes care to use fresh ingredients. Luckily, she has many relatives who help her run the shop, so she is never alone. People from different nationalities visit her store every day, and she treats everyone with respect.

Maria é a proprietária de um pequeno negócio em seu bairro. Ela vende comida diretamente para seus clientes e sempre cuida para usar ingredientes frescos. Por sorte, ela tem muitos parentes que a ajudam a administrar a loja, então ela nunca está sozinha. Pessoas de diferentes nacionalidades visitam sua loja todos os dias, e ela trata a todos com respeito.

One day, a local orchestra gave an impressive concert in the neighborhood park. Nobody was rude to anyone, and prejudice seemed to disappear for a few hours. Maria still talks about that night and says it was worth it to close her business early and go listen. She believes that music is another way to connect people from all walks of life.

Um dia, uma orquestra local deu um concerto impressionante no parque do bairro. Ninguém foi grosseiro com ninguém, e o preconceito pareceu desaparecer por algumas horas. Maria ainda fala sobre aquela noite e diz que valeu a pena fechar seu negócio mais cedo e ir ouvir. Ela acredita que a música é mais uma forma de conectar pessoas de todas as origens.
