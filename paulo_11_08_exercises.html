Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Can you describe the weather in your city right now? What is it usually like throughout the year?
Right now, the weather in my city is hot and sunny. It can get very humid in the summer, but in winter it can be cool and rainy. I should always bring an umbrella when the weather looks bad.
2. What should a person always bring when they travel? Why?
A person should always bring their wallet, an umbrella, and their phone. The wallet is important for payments everywhere you go, the umbrella is useful when the weather is bad, and the phone is needed for communication and navigation.
3. What could you do when you were a child that you cannot do now? Or what can you do now that you couldn't do before?
When I was a child, I could climb trees everywhere in my neighborhood. I could also reach the top of the ladder in my grandfather's garage easily. Now I can't do those things, but I can drive a car and I will keep learning new skills.
