Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Do you think it is easy to retire early in Brazil? Why or why not?
No, I don't think it is easy to retire early in Brazil. Many people work for 30 or 35 years before they retire. The retirement money is not always enough to cover all the bills and daily expenses.
2. What do you do when someone tries to fool you or cheat you in a deal?
When someone tries to fool me, I stay calm and ask for all the receipts and contracts. I read everything carefully before I sign or pay. If I find out that something is wrong, I do not proceed with the deal.
3. Is it a good idea to borrow money from friends or family? What can go wrong?
I think it is risky to borrow money from friends or family. Sometimes people forget to pay back the debt, and it creates conflict. It is better to set a clear date to pay back and always keep it.

The Slippery Deal

Marco worked at a small accounting firm in Chicago for many years. Every day, he helped his clients organize their receipts, pay their bills, and manage their debt. He knew that money problems cause a lot of stress, so he always tried to give honest advice. One afternoon, a slippery salesman walked into his office and tried to fool him into signing a bad contract.

Marco trabalhou em um pequeno escritório de contabilidade em Chicago por muitos anos. Todo dia, ele ajudava seus clientes a organizar os recibos, pagar as contas e gerenciar as dívidas. Ele sabia que problemas com dinheiro causam muito estresse, então sempre tentava dar conselhos honestos. Uma tarde, um vendedor escorregadio entrou no seu escritório e tentou enganá-lo para assinar um contrato ruim.

Marco was not a fool. He read every line of the contract and found out that the salesman wanted to charge him twice for the same service. The outcome was not good for the salesman — Marco reported him to the authorities. After that difficult situation, Marco decided to retire and enjoy his retirement in peace. He lent his office to a young accountant and never looked back.

Marco não era tolo. Ele leu cada linha do contrato e descobriu que o vendedor queria cobrar duas vezes pelo mesmo serviço. O resultado final não foi bom para o vendedor — Marco o denunciou às autoridades. Após aquela situação difícil, Marco decidiu se aposentar e aproveitar sua aposentadoria em paz. Ele emprestou seu escritório a um jovem contador e nunca mais olhou para trás.

🎯 Múltipla Escolha

1. B) Emprestar
2. C) Debt
3. C) Descobrir algo
4. C) Escorregadio, liso
5. B) 'To fool' means to trick someone in general; 'to cheat on' means to be unfaithful to a romantic partner.
