Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Do you know what sleep paralysis is? Did it ever happen to you?
Sleep paralysis is when you wake up but you cannot move your body. Yes, it happened to me once. It was very scary — I could not scream or move at all.
2. What does a caregiver do in a typical day? Describe the routine.
A caregiver wakes up early and helps the elder with daily activities. She showers them, prepares their meals, and gives them medicine. It is a hard but very important job.
3. Is there a bakery close to your home? What do you usually buy there?
Yes, there is a bakery close to my home. I usually buy fresh bread and cheese bread there. I go almost every morning before work.
