Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. She sick yesterday, so she stayed home.
Use o passado de 'to feel' (sentir). *FELT* = sentiu/estava se sentindo.
2. We to the beach last weekend.
Use o passado de 'to go' (ir). *WENT* = foi/foram.
3. He searched everywhere and finally his keys under the couch.
Use o passado de 'to find' (encontrar). *FOUND* = encontrou.
4. My dad to work every day last year.
Use o passado de 'to drive' (dirigir). *DROVE* = dirigiu.
5. I was very tired after the long trip, so I and went straight to bed.
Expressão com TAKE que significa tomar banho: *TOOK A SHOWER*.
6. My friends were hungry, so I cooked for
Pronome objeto de 'they'. *THEM* = para eles/elas.

Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. What did you eat and drink yesterday? Describe your meals using past tense.
Yesterday, I ate rice and beans for lunch and drank orange juice. At night, I ate a sandwich and drank water. I felt very full after dinner!
2. Tell me about a trip you took. Where did you go? What did you see? How did you feel?
I went to São Paulo last year with my family. We saw many tall buildings and took a lot of pictures. I felt excited but also tired. We stayed there for three days.
3. Did you try to cook something recently? What did you make? Did you need any help?
Yes, I tried to make a cake last weekend. I needed eggs and sugar, but I didn't have enough sugar. I decided to go to the store and get more. The cake was good!

A Weekend Without Plans

Last Saturday, Maria decided to stay home without any plans. She felt tired after a long week at work, so she wanted to rest. She made coffee, found a good book, and read it all morning. Around noon, she thought about cooking something special, so she went to the store and got everything she needed.

No último sábado, Maria decidiu ficar em casa sem nenhum plano. Ela se sentiu cansada depois de uma longa semana no trabalho, então queria descansar. Ela fez café, achou um bom livro e leu a manhã toda. Por volta do meio-dia, pensou em cozinhar algo especial, então foi ao mercado e pegou tudo o que precisava.

In the afternoon, Maria tried a new recipe and made a delicious soup. She took a picture of it and sent it to her friends. They saw the photo and complained that she didn't invite them! Maria laughed and said she would cook for them next time. That day, she felt happy and free, even without going anywhere.

À tarde, Maria tentou uma nova receita e fez uma sopa deliciosa. Ela tirou uma foto e mandou para seus amigos. Eles viram a foto e reclamaram que ela não os convidou! Maria riu e disse que cozinharia para eles da próxima vez. Naquele dia, ela se sentiu feliz e livre, mesmo sem ir a lugar nenhum.
