Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. What do you usually do on a holiday? Use 'after that' in your answer.
On a holiday, I usually sleep late. After that, I spend time with my family and we sit down to watch a movie together.
2. Describe the last time you went to a clothing store. What did you pick up?
I went to a clothing store last weekend. I picked up two shirts and a pair of jeans. I spent about two hours there and showed everything to my friend after that.
3. Do you think it is easy to catch a thief? Say 'I think so' or 'I don't think so' and explain why.
I don't think so. Thieves are very fast and they know how to hide. The police need a lot of time to find them and show the evidence.

A Busy Holiday at the Clothing Store

Last Monday was a holiday, so Maria decided to go to the clothing store near her house. She picked up a few dresses and sat down to think about which one she liked the most. After that, she showed the dresses to her friend and asked for her opinion. Her friend said, 'I think so,' when Maria asked if the blue dress looked good on her.

Na última segunda-feira era feriado, então Maria decidiu ir à loja de roupas perto de sua casa. Ela pegou alguns vestidos e sentou para pensar em qual gostava mais. Depois disso, ela mostrou os vestidos para a amiga e pediu a opinião dela. A amiga disse 'acho que sim' quando Maria perguntou se o vestido azul ficava bem nela.

Maria spent a lot of money that day, but she was very happy with everything. On the way home, she saw a thief run out of another store with a bag. She thought about what to do and decided to call the police right away. The police took the thief away quickly. It was a very exciting holiday for Maria.

Maria gastou muito dinheiro naquele dia, mas ficou muito feliz com tudo. No caminho para casa, ela viu um ladrão sair correndo de outra loja com uma bolsa. Ela pensou no que fazer e decidiu ligar para a polícia imediatamente. A polícia levou o ladrão embora rapidamente. Foi um feriado muito emocionante para Maria.
