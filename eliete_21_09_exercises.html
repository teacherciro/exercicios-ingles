Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. I work at a dance company, but I also travel .
Use *A LOT* (muito) no final da frase, antes do ponto.
2. I meet new dancers and musicians in every city.
Use *ALSO* (também) entre o sujeito e o verbo.
3. Last month, Amy in Rome.
Passado simples de WORK = *WORKED*
4. He drives buses every day. He is a .
Adicione -R ao verbo *DRIVE* para formar a profissão.
5. She is happy. She dances every day.
Use *VERY* (muito) antes de adjetivos.
6. I eat a lot, and I travel a lot,
Use *TOO* (também) no final da frase.
