2
Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. We all in the park to celebrate her birthday.
Use o passado do verbo *GATHER* — significa 'se juntar'.
2. The market had different types of Japanese souvenirs.
'Diversos' ou 'vários' em inglês é *SEVERAL*.
3. I bought a leather wallet as a souvenir.
A carteira era cheia de cores — use *COLORFUL* (grafia americana, sem 'u').
4. The winter jacket was very — perfect for walking in the snow.
O casaco era grosso e pesado — use *THICK* (espesso/grosso).
5. bag do you prefer — the leather one or the colorful one?
Use *WHICH* para perguntar 'qual' entre opções específicas.
6. She carried the heavy luggage with her friends all the way to the station.
'Junto' em inglês é *TOGETHER*. 'Together with' significa 'junto com'.

4
Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Have you ever missed someone a lot after they moved away? What did you do to feel better?
Yes, I really missed my best friend when she moved to another city. We gathered together during the holidays and I carried a lot of gifts for her. Seeing her again made me feel so much better.
2. If you could buy a few souvenirs from Japan, which ones would you choose and why?
I would buy a few colorful ceramic pieces and a thick leather wallet. I notice that Japanese souvenirs are always very detailed and carefully made, which makes them really special.
3. Describe a colorful market or fair you have visited. What did you notice there?
I visited a street market in São Paulo that had several colorful stalls. I noticed they sold raw ingredients, thick handmade fabrics, and leather bags together in the same area. It was a really fun experience!
