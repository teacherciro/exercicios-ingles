A Scary Night at the Hospital

Last Monday, Carlos fell down the stairs at his house and hurt his arm badly. His family took him to the emergency room right away. The doctor did an X-ray and saw that the bone in his arm was broken. Carlos also had a large bruise near his elbow from the fall.

Na última segunda-feira, Carlos caiu da escada de sua casa e machucou o braço com força. Sua família o levou ao pronto-socorro imediatamente. O médico fez um raio-X e viu que o osso do braço estava quebrado. Carlos também tinha um grande roxo perto do cotovelo por causa da queda.

Because his condition was serious, the doctors moved Carlos to the ICU — the Intensive Care Unit. The nurses in the ICU took good care of him all night. The next morning, Carlos felt much better and the doctors said his bone was going to heal well. His bruise was still there, but the pain was much less. His whole family was very relieved.

Como sua condição era grave, os médicos transferiram Carlos para a UTI — a Unidade de Terapia Intensiva. As enfermeiras da UTI cuidaram muito bem dele a noite toda. Na manhã seguinte, Carlos se sentiu muito melhor e os médicos disseram que o osso ia se recuperar bem. O roxo ainda estava lá, mas a dor era muito menor. Toda a sua família ficou muito aliviada.
