Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. What did you like to do during vacations when you were a child?
When I was a child, I loved to go to the beach with my family. We ate a lot of spaghetti and soup at home, and I always played with my favorite toys. I really miss those times!
2. Do you think it is important to take care of your health? What do you do to take care of yourself?
Yes, I believe it is very important. I try to take a walk every day and drink a lot of water. I also never forget to take my medicines when I need them, and I always pay attention to what I eat.
3. Tell me about someone or something you really miss. Who or what is it, and why do you miss it?
I really miss my grandmother. She gave me a lot of love and always made the best soup I have ever eaten. She managed to make every holiday feel special for our whole family. I think about her all the time.
