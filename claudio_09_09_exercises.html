Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. I haven't booked my ticket yet. I need to do it right now!
Palavra que significa 'voo': *FLIGHT*
2. She always carries her to school because it has everything she needs.
Objeto que carregamos nas costas: *BACKPACK*
3. It's a beautiful day — it's and warm, perfect for a road trip!
Adjetivo que descreve um dia com muito sol: *SUNNY*
4. Don't worry, Claudio! You are for the test — you studied all week.
Adjetivo que significa estar pronto para algo: *PREPARED*
5. I feel nervous before every flight, even after traveling many times.
Palavra usada em frases afirmativas para dizer 'ainda': *STILL*
6. My younger sibling is so — she always wants to joke around and never takes things seriously.
Adjetivo para alguém que gosta de brincadeiras: *PLAYFUL*

4
Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Do you have any siblings? Are they playful or more serious? Are they generous?
Yes, I have one sibling. My brother is very playful — he always makes jokes. He is also very generous and always helps me when I need something.
2. What do you do right now to learn English? Do you still feel nervous when you speak?
Right now, I take English classes and try to practice every day. I still feel a little nervous when I speak, but I feel more prepared than before.
3. Imagine you are late for a flight. What things do you put in your backpack? How do you feel?
I put my key, my passport, and some snacks in my backpack because I might get hungry. I feel very stressed when I am late — I don't like to freeze under pressure!
