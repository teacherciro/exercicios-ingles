A Dream Around the World

Maria and her husband always wanted to travel around the world. At the beginning of the year, they decided to leave their jobs and discover new places. They found out that some countries are very different from Brazil, and they felt happy every single day of the trip.

Maria e o marido sempre quiseram viajar pelo mundo. No início do ano, eles decidiram deixar os empregos e descobrir novos lugares. Eles descobriram que alguns países são muito diferentes do Brasil, e se sentiram felizes todos os dias da viagem.

The couple also needed to buy some lenses for their camera before leaving. At the same time, they found out that some places are only open at certain times of the year. They left home in January and discovered beautiful places everywhere. It was difficult to know which country was their favorite!

O casal também precisou comprar algumas lentes para a câmera antes de sair. Ao mesmo tempo, eles descobriram que alguns lugares só abrem em determinadas épocas do ano. Eles saíram de casa em janeiro e descobriram lugares lindos por toda parte. Era difícil saber qual país era o favorito deles!
