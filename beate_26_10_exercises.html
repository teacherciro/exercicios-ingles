Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. Let me — I need a moment to process this.
Use o verbo *THINK* (pensar) logo após 'Let me'.
2. There a great sitcom on TV tonight.
Use *IS* porque 'a sitcom' é singular.
3. From time to , my son visits me in the capital.
Complete a expressão 'from time to ___' com a palavra *TIME*.
4. I understood the lesson. It was not difficult!
Use o advérbio *ALREADY* (já) entre o sujeito 'I' e o verbo.
5. The capital city is very . There are millions of people everywhere!
Use o adjetivo *CHAOTIC* (caótico) para descrever a cidade.
6. Her grandfather last year. It was a tragic moment for the whole family.
Use o phrasal verb *PASS AWAY* no passado simples (falecer).

Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Do you watch TV shows or sitcoms? Tell me about your favorite one.
Yes, I watch TV shows from time to time. My favorite sitcom is Friends because the episodes are almost always funny. The last time I watched it, I already knew all the jokes — I got it every time!
2. Use 'There is' or 'There are' to describe three things about your city or neighborhood.
There is a library near my house. There are many children in my street. There isn't a big park, but there are some small ones. Is there a good coffee shop near you?
3. Tell me about the last time something surprised you. Use 'I got it' or 'I see' in your answer.
The last time I was surprised was in my English class. My teacher explained 'There is' and 'There are', and suddenly I got it! I see now that it depends on singular or plural. It was almost like having a shield against mistakes!

A Chaotic and Wonderful Capital

My son lives in the capital city with his daughter — my granddaughter. From time to time, I visit them, and it is always an adventure. The city is almost always chaotic and crazy, but there is so much to love about it. There are great libraries, parks, and children playing everywhere. There is always something new to discover!

Meu filho mora na cidade capital com sua filha — minha neta. De tempo em tempo, eu os visito, e é sempre uma aventura. A cidade é quase sempre caótica e louca, mas tem muito para amar nela. Tem ótimas bibliotecas, parques e crianças brincando por todo lugar. Sempre tem algo novo para descobrir!

The last time I visited, we watched a sitcom together. There were almost twenty episodes, and some of them were tragic, but most were funny. I already understood the story after the first episode — I got it right away! My son said 'I see!' every time something surprising happened. There is a real possibility we will do it again next year. I can't wait!

A última vez que visitei, assistimos a uma série de comédia juntos. Tinha quase vinte episódios, e alguns deles eram trágicos, mas a maioria era engraçada. Eu já entendia a história depois do primeiro episódio — entendi na hora! Meu filho dizia 'Eu entendo!' toda vez que algo surpreendente acontecia. Tem uma real possibilidade de que faremos isso de novo no próximo ano. Mal posso esperar!
