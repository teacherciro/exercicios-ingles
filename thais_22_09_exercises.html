Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Tell me about your morning routine. Use at least three frequency adverbs (always, usually, sometimes, never, often) and verbs from the lesson.
I always wake up early and usually have breakfast at home. I sometimes drink coffee and read the news before work. I almost never go to bed late, so I never feel tired in the morning.
2. What did you do last weekend? Use at least four verbs in the past tense from the lesson.
Last weekend, I went to the market and bought some fresh vegetables. I cooked dinner at home and ate with my family. Later, I listened to music and slept early. It was a great weekend!
3. Suggest three activities for tonight using 'Let's'. Use 'have' to replace 'eat' or 'drink' in at least one suggestion.
Let's have dinner at that new Italian restaurant downtown. Let's have a glass of wine after we eat. Let's walk home after dinner — it's a beautiful night!
