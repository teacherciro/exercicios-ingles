Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. I sick after I drank that strange juice.
Use o passado do verbo GET para expressar que ficou doente: *GOT*
2. She to the gym three times a week.
Use o presente simples do verbo GO com 'she' — lembre do -S: *GOES*
3. I eat fast food. Maybe once a month.
Este advérbio significa 'quase nunca': *HARDLY EVER*
4. Paulo his own drums when he was young.
Use o passado do verbo SELL (vender): *SOLD*
5. How often you drive to work?
Perguntas no presente simples com 'you' precisam do auxiliar: *DO*
6. I have breakfast before I go to work.
Este advérbio significa 'quase sempre': *ALMOST ALWAYS*

Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. How often do you eat fast food? Tell me how many times a week or month.
I almost never eat fast food. Maybe once a month. I prefer to cook at home because I know it is better for my weight.
2. What do you usually do on weekends? Use at least one frequency adverb in your answer.
I usually play video games on weekends. I sometimes watch movies with my family, and I almost always have lunch with my parents on Sundays.
3. Tell me about something strange you saw or found recently.
I found a strange book in my room last week. I did not know it was there. It was very old and I thought it belonged to my grandfather.
