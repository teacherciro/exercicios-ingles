Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. My brother is very confident. He considers the best salesman in the company.
Pronome reflexivo para 'he'. Lembre-se da família: myself, yourself, *HIMSELF*...
2. When I was a child, I eat a lot of candy every day, but now I don't.
Use essa expressão para falar de um hábito do *PASSADO* que não acontece mais. Nunca use no presente!
3. The governor sent all the important documents to the before the election.
Cargo político federal: a pessoa que representa um estado no *SENADO*.
4. Be careful with that — it's very sharp and dangerous!
Utensílio de cozinha com lâmina. No plural, vira *KNIVES* (regra F/FE → VES).
5. I go to the post office every Monday to send packages to my family.
Use essa palavra para hábitos do *PRESENTE*. Não confunda com 'used to', que é sempre passado!
6. She didn't have health when she was unemployed, so she had to pay for everything herself.
Palavra que significa *SEGURO* (de saúde, de carro, de vida, etc.).

4
Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Did you use to follow politics when you were younger? How has your interest changed over the years?
I used to watch the news every night with my parents, but now I usually just read about politics on my phone. I didn't use to understand the difference between senators and congressmen, but now I do. I consider myself more informed about right-wing and left-wing ideas than before.
2. If you could consider yourself an expert in something, what would it be and why? Use 'I consider myself' in your answer.
I consider myself a pretty good cook. I usually prepare dinner for my family on weekends. I didn't use to know how to cook at all, but I taught myself little by little. Now I can use a knife like a professional chef!
3. In your opinion, what qualities should a good governor or senator have? Do you think right-wing and left-wing candidates can ever work together?
I think a good senator should consider the needs of all citizens in their zone, not just the ones who voted for them. I used to think right-wing and left-wing politicians could never agree, but now I usually see them cooperating on important issues like health insurance for everyone.
