Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Do you think being a caregiver is a difficult job? Why or why not?
Yes, I think it is a very difficult job because caregivers need to be strong and patient every day. They take care of people who need a lot of help, and that is emotionally demanding.
2. Do you know anyone who is very dramatic? What does that person do when something small goes wrong?
Yes, my friend is very dramatic. When something small goes wrong, she cries and talks about it for hours. It is funny sometimes, but also a little exhausting.
3. What is your favorite drink at a bar or restaurant? Does it have mint, grapes, or any other fruit in it?
My favorite drink is a lemonade with mint and grapes. I love the fresh taste of mint, and the grapes make it a little sweet. It is very refreshing.
