Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. That's my brother over there. Do you know ?
Use o pronome objeto para 'he'. Dica: *HIM*
2. I speak Portuguese and English. I learned both when I was young.
Use o auxiliar que expressa CAPACIDADE no presente. Dica: *CAN*
3. She come to the party tomorrow. She has to work all day.
Forma negativa contraída de WILL. Dica: *WON'T*
4. This store has a great on laptops this week. You should check it out!
Palavra que significa promoção ou oferta especial. Dica: *OFFER*
5. You study more if you want to pass the test. I'm serious.
Use o auxiliar de RECOMENDAÇÃO. Dica: *SHOULD*
6. I don't know very well. We only met last week.
Pronome objeto para 'they'. Dica: *THEM*

Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. What can you do well? What couldn't you do when you were a child?
I can speak English and drive a car. I couldn't drive when I was a child, and I couldn't cook either.
2. You see a product on sale at the store. What would you say to a friend about it? Use 'on sale', 'only', or 'just'.
Hey, look at this! These sneakers are on sale. They are just $40 today only. You should buy them!
3. Give one piece of advice to a friend who wants to learn English. Use 'should' or 'shouldn't'.
You should practice every day. You shouldn't translate everything in your head — just try to think in English.
