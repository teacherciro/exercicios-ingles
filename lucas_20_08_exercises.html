Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. I'm sorry, but the boots you want are not in your size right now.
Esta palavra significa 'disponível'. Em maiúsculas: *AVAILABLE*
2. We need to leave now if we want to get to the gas station .
Esta expressão de duas palavras significa 'a tempo, pontualmente'. Em maiúsculas: *ON TIME*
3. The grocery store is five minutes from the bus stop.
Esta palavra aparece antes de números e significa 'cerca de, por volta de'. Em maiúsculas: *AROUND*
4. I thought that town was boring, but it has amazing destinations for tourists.
Esta palavra significa 'na verdade' e é usada para corrigir uma ideia anterior. Em maiúsculas: *ACTUALLY*
5. It's difficult to talk on the phone and drive .
Esta expressão de quatro palavras significa 'ao mesmo tempo'. Em maiúsculas: *AT THE SAME TIME*
6. , did you meet my godson at the party room last Saturday?
Esta expressão de três palavras significa 'aliás, a propósito' e é usada no início da frase. Em maiúsculas: *BY THE WAY*

4
Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Think about a small town you have visited or would like to visit. How far is it from where you live, and what can you find there?
I once visited a small town called Tiradentes. It's around three hours from Belo Horizonte by car. You can find beautiful old churches, local restaurants, and lots of cool stuff to buy, like handmade boots and traditional food. By the way, the party rooms there are amazing — they host great festivals!
2. If you could choose any destination in the world to travel to, where would you go? What questions would you ask to get around the city?
I would choose Japan. I'm getting excited just thinking about it! I'd ask things like 'How do I get to the subway station?' or 'Where is the closest grocery store?' I think people there are very polite and would help me find my way around.
3. Describe a typical busy day in your life. Do you manage to do everything on time? Is it hard to do two things at the same time?
My days are pretty hectic. I try to arrive at work on time, but sometimes I'm about ten minutes late. I usually have to answer emails and attend meetings at the same time, which is stressful. Actually, the hardest part is that I also study English around three times a week — but I love it!
