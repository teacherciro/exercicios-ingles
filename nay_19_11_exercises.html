2. How do you feel about going to school every day?
I don't like going to school every day. It is a little boring sometimes. But I know I need to go because it is important for my future.
3. If you could visit one country, where would you go — with your family or alone?
I would love to visit Italy one day! I think I would go with my family because it is more fun. But maybe I would also like to go alone and paint beautiful places there.

Leitura

📚 Leia o texto abaixo. Use as palavras e estruturas da aula. Clique em "Ver tradução" quando quiser conferir!

Candies, Colors, and Dreams

Nay loves eating candies, but she doesn't eat them every day. She only enjoys her favorite sweets when she is at home, relaxing and feeling happy. School is not her favorite place — she doesn't like going there, but she knows it is important, so she goes every single day. Sometimes, she sits in class and thinks about being home instead.

Nay adora comer balas, mas ela não come todo dia. Ela só aproveita seus doces favoritos quando está em casa, relaxando e se sentindo feliz. A escola não é seu lugar favorito — ela não gosta de ir lá, mas sabe que é importante, então vai todos os dias. Às vezes, ela fica sentada na sala de aula pensando em estar em casa.

One day, Nay wants to stay at home the whole year, doing what she loves most: painting. She dreams of visiting Italy with her family, walking through beautiful streets and seeing amazing art. But deep in her heart, she thinks she would prefer to go alone — just her, her paintbrush, and the colors of Italy.

Um dia, Nay quer ficar em casa o ano inteiro, fazendo o que mais ama: pintar. Ela sonha em visitar a Itália com sua família, caminhando por ruas bonitas e vendo arte incrível. Mas no fundo do coração, ela acha que preferiria ir sozinha — só ela, seu pincel e as cores da Itália.
