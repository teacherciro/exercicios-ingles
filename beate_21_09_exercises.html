2
Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. I have breakfast every single morning.
Advérbio que significa 'sempre' — 100% de frequência: *ALWAYS*
2. How do you work out?
Expressão WH- para perguntar frequência: 'HOW ___'. Complete com *OFTEN*
3. She loves children and works at a school. She is a great .
Profissão: verbo TEACH + ER = *TEACHER*
4. I go to the gym a week — on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.
Para três repetições, usamos o número + TIMES: *THREE TIMES*
5. Usually lunch at home, but sometimes I go to a restaurant.
Quando 'Usually' começa a frase, vem sujeito + verbo logo depois: *USUALLY* + I + have
6. I hardly ever eat for dinner — I prefer steak.
Fruto do mar que significa camarão: *SHRIMP*

Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. How often do you have breakfast? What do you usually eat or drink?
I almost always have breakfast. I usually eat bread and drink coffee in the morning.
2. What do you hardly ever eat? Why?
I hardly ever eat shrimp because I don't really like seafood. I almost always prefer chicken or beef.
3. How many times a week do you work out? What do you usually do?
I work out three times a week. I usually go to the gym on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.
