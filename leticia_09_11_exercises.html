Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Do you think Jimmy is telling the truth? Why or why not?
No, I don't think Jimmy is telling the truth. His story changes too many times and he can't remember important details, like the name of the movie he watched. That's very suspicious!
2. Think about last Saturday. What did you do? Use at least three verbs in the simple past.
Last Saturday, I woke up late and watched a movie. After that, I visited a friend and we had lunch together. In the evening, I cleaned my room and went to bed early. It was a great day!
3. What is a useful way to remember new English vocabulary? Explain your method.
I think a useful way to remember new words is to write them in sentences. That way, I can see how they are used in context, and it's much easier to remember them. Using flashcards is also a great way to practice!

Jimmy's Very Long Saturday

Detective Roberts had a lot of questions for Jimmy. The detective wanted to know about Jimmy's Saturday night. Jimmy said he was at home in the morning and watched a baseball game on TV. For him, there was no better way to spend a Saturday morning than cheering for his favorite team.

O Detetive Roberts tinha muitas perguntas para Jimmy. O detetive queria saber sobre o sábado à noite de Jimmy. Jimmy disse que estava em casa de manhã e assistiu a um jogo de beisebol na TV. Para ele, não havia jeito melhor de passar uma manhã de sábado do que torcer pelo seu time favorito.

Later that day, Jimmy went to his martial arts class, like he always did on Saturdays. After class, he visited his friends Jordan and Stephanie Smith. He went home at 6:00, cleaned the house, and then watched a fantastic and exciting movie. He went to bed at exactly 10:30. It sucks when you have to explain everything you did all day — but that's what happens when a detective comes knocking at your door!

Mais tarde naquele dia, Jimmy foi para sua aula de artes marciais, como sempre fazia aos sábados. Depois da aula, ele visitou seus amigos Jordan e Stephanie Smith. Ele foi para casa às 6h, limpou a casa e depois assistiu a um filme fantástico e emocionante. Ele foi dormir exatamente às 10:30. É uma droga ter que explicar tudo o que fez durante o dia — mas é o que acontece quando um detetive vem bater na sua porta!
