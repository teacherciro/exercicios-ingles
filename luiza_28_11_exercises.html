Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Tell me about a friend or family member you need to catch up with. Why do you need to catch up?
I need to catch up with my cousin. She moved to another city last year, and we don't talk very often. I want to hear all about her new job and her new neighborhood.
2. What is your favorite trip down memory lane? Describe a memory that makes you feel nostalgic.
My favorite trip down memory lane is when I look at old school photos. I remember my old friends and the fun things we did together during recess. It always makes me smile.
3. Do you watch soap operas? Do you think they are a good way to practice English? Why or why not?
Yes, I watch soap operas sometimes. I think they are a good way to practice English because the actors speak clearly and the situations are easy to follow. But sometimes the drama is too exaggerated and hard to believe!

Sunrise, Soap Operas, and Old Friends

Ana woke up at sunup and sat by the window with a cup of coffee. She felt a little disheartened that morning — her company opened three new branches, and there was so much stuff to organize. The extra work left her stressed and exhausted, and she missed her old friend Carol more than ever.

Ana acordou no nascer do sol e sentou perto da janela com uma xícara de café. Ela se sentiu um pouco desanimada naquela manhã — sua empresa abriu três novas filiais, e havia tantas coisas para organizar. O trabalho extra a deixou estressada e exausta, e ela estava com saudades da velha amiga Carol mais do que nunca.

That afternoon, Carol sent Ana a message: 'Let's meet and catch up!' They sat at their old café and looked at photos on Carol's phone — it was a real trip down memory lane. They laughed, talked, and even watched a funny soap opera clip together. When they finally said farewell, Ana felt like a completely new person.

Naquela tarde, Carol mandou uma mensagem para Ana: 'Vamos nos encontrar e colocar o papo em dia!' Elas sentaram no café antigo e olharam para fotos no celular de Carol — foi uma verdadeira viagem ao passado. Riram, conversaram e até assistiram juntas a um clipe engraçado de novela. Quando finalmente se despediram, Ana se sentiu uma pessoa completamente nova.
