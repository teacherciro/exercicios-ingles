Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. What medical specialty do you visit when you have a bone problem, and what kind of advice does this doctor usually give?
I visit an orthopedist when I have a bone problem. This doctor usually gives advice about rest, exercise, and diet. Sometimes the orthopedist also uses clinical research to find the best treatment for the patient.
2. Do you prefer to buy things in installments or pay all at once? Does the level of interests change your decision?
I prefer to pay in installments when the interests are low because it helps me manage my money. When the interests are high, I try to pay everything at once to save money. It always depends on the price and the interest rate.
3. Compare your hometown to the city where you live now. What are the main differences?
When I compare my hometown to the city where I live now, I see many differences. My hometown is small and quiet, but the city is busy and full of opportunities. I depend on the city for work, but I love to visit my hometown on vacation.

The Orthopedist from a Small Town

Dr. Maria is an orthopedist from a small hometown in Brazil. She uses clinical research as a tool to find the best treatments for bone problems. Every week, she compares new medical techniques to old ones and says to her patients: 'Your health depends on good habits and regular exercise.'

A Dra. Maria é ortopedista de uma cidade natal pequena no Brasil. Ela usa a pesquisa clínica como ferramenta para encontrar os melhores tratamentos para problemas nos ossos. Toda semana, ela compara novas técnicas médicas às antigas e diz aos seus pacientes: 'Sua saúde depende de bons hábitos e exercícios regulares.'

One of her patients needed surgery, but the costs were high. The patient decided to pay in installments because the interests at the bank were low that month. After the surgery, the doctor confirmed that the patient was alive and healthy. Dr. Maria smiled and said to her team: 'This is why I love my work — every patient alive is a victory.'

Um de seus pacientes precisou de cirurgia, mas os custos eram altos. O paciente decidiu pagar em parcelas porque os juros do banco estavam baixos naquele mês. Após a cirurgia, a médica confirmou que o paciente estava vivo e saudável. A Dra. Maria sorriu e disse à sua equipe: 'É por isso que amo meu trabalho — cada paciente vivo é uma vitória.'
