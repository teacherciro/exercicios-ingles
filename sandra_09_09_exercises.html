Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. Do you think the shared economy is worth it? What are the advantages and disadvantages?
Yes, I think the shared economy is worth it. Apps like Uber are profitable and very convenient. Instead of buying a car, people can just pay a small fee for a ride. However, some people worry about safety and high insurance deductibles in case of an accident.
2. What do you do when something worries you? How do you avoid making things worse?
When something worries me, I try to stay calm and talk to a friend. I avoid making things worse by thinking carefully before I say anything. I even write my thoughts down sometimes to help me feel better and more focused.
3. Did you ever see a cracked windshield? What happened?
Yes, I saw a cracked windshield once. A rock hit the car glass on the highway and it cracked immediately. The driver was very worried about the repair cost and the insurance deductible. It was a very stressful situation, and I didn't even know the repair was so expensive.
