Complete as Frases
✏️
📝 Preencha os espaços e clique em "Verificar"!
1. After 50 years as a pilot, he finally for the last time and retired.
O piloto pousou pela última vez. Use o passado do verbo *LAND*.
2. He was born in the northeast, so his is far from the big city.
A cidade onde ele nasceu e cresceu. Use a palavra *HOMETOWN*.
3. He left the countryside at age 18, and he has lived downtown.
A expressão indica que algo continua desde aquele momento. Use *SINCE THEN*.
4. My mother always asks me to take out the garbage and finish my before watching TV.
São as tarefas domésticas que você precisa fazer em casa. Use a palavra *CHORES*.
5. The plane from the small airport in the countryside right on time.
O avião decolou. Use o passado do phrasal verb *TAKE OFF*.
6. After retiring, he invested all his savings in in his hometown.
Ele investiu em imóveis. Use a expressão *REAL ESTATE*.

4
Perguntas Abertas
💬
🖊️ Responda em inglês! Clique em "Ver modelo" para se inspirar.
1. What kind of person do you think someone needs to be to work as a pilot for 50 years?
I think a pilot needs to be very disciplined and calm. They must not worry too much under pressure and have to be straight about safety rules. It takes a really special kind of person to do that job for so long.
2. Would you prefer to live downtown or in the countryside? Why?
I would prefer to live downtown because it is close to everything, like stores, schools, and churches. However, I still miss the quiet life of the countryside. Since I moved to the city, things have been very different for me.
3. What chores do you do at home? Do you ever mess up the house?
I usually take out the garbage and clean my room every day. Sometimes I mess up the kitchen when I cook, but I always clean it afterwards. My mom used to worry about me at first, but not anymore.
