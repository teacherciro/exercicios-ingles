2. How long do first person shooter games usually last? Do you play them?

The Minister's Plan to Fight Booze

Last year, the minister announced a plan to increase the tax on booze. He said the government wanted to decrease the consumption of alcohol in the country. Many people did not think it was fair, but the minister argued it was necessary for public health.

No ano passado, o ministro anunciou um plano para aumentar o imposto sobre bebidas alcoólicas. Ele disse que o governo queria diminuir o consumo de álcool no país. Muitas pessoas não acharam justo, mas o ministro argumentou que era necessário para a saúde pública.

The plan lasted for one full year, and the results were positive. The number of people who consume booze decreased by 15%. The minister said it was fair to continue the high taxes, and the government decided to increase them even more. The new policy changed life in every block of every city in the country.

O plano durou um ano completo e os resultados foram positivos. O número de pessoas que consome bebidas alcoólicas diminuiu 15%. O ministro disse que era justo manter os impostos altos, e o governo decidiu aumentá-los ainda mais. A nova política mudou a vida em cada quarteirão de cada cidade do país.
